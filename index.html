How to use this assessment

This assessment helps leaders see how learning moves through an organization—and where learning may be getting stuck.

The learning structures come from the five published articles in the Organizational Learning Infrastructure series. They are a menu of possible structures, not a checklist every organization must use.

Important: This is a developmental tool, not a validated psychometric instrument. Use the results to support reflection, dialogue, prioritization, and organizational development—not to rank organizations or produce a single overall score.

What to do with the results

  1. Look for patterns, not winners and losers.
  2. Look for bottlenecks. Where is learning accumulating without moving to the next phase?
  3. Validate the picture with other perspectives.
  4. Choose one to three development priorities. Do not turn 88 structures into 88 projects.
  5. Run a small experiment. Build or strengthen one structure, establish a cadence, and observe what happens.
  6. Reassess. The infrastructure itself should become part of the organization's learning cycle.

The first step in building an organizational learning infrastructure may be learning how your organization currently learns.

Submit Your Assessment

Submitting your results is optional. You can keep your assessment private or submit an anonymous copy for archival and future aggregate learning.

Privacy: Do not enter confidential, personally identifiable, or sensitive organizational information in the evidence/notes fields if you choose to submit your results.
